Privacy policy

What data we use on bweb.uy, what we use it for and how to ask us to correct or delete it.

Who we are

Bweb is a web design studio in Uruguay. We’re responsible for the data you leave on this site. For any question about your data, message us on WhatsApp at +598 99 788 934.

What data we receive

  • Contact form: your name, your email, your WhatsApp number (if you add it), your business and whatever you tell us. It reaches us by email through Web3Forms, the service we use to send the form.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us, we receive your number and your messages. WhatsApp is a Meta service.
  • Analytics and ads: we use Google Analytics and the Meta pixel to know how many people visit the site and tap the buttons (for example, “Chat on WhatsApp” or “Send message”) and to show Bweb ads on Facebook and Instagram. Both use cookies and browser identifiers.
  • Language: if you choose to see the site in English or Spanish, we remember it in your browser. That choice stays on your device and doesn’t reach us.

What we use it for

  • Replying to your inquiries and coordinating the work you hire us for.
  • Measuring what works on the site and in our ads.
  • Showing you Bweb ads on Facebook and Instagram. If you’re a client, we may use your WhatsApp number to build audiences on Meta: it’s uploaded encoded and Meta doesn’t use it to contact you.

We don’t sell your data or share it for any other purpose.

Third-party services

The site uses services from other companies that may receive technical data about your visit, such as your IP address or your browser:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and serves the icons.
  • Google: the fonts and Google Analytics, which we use to measure visits to the site and which uses cookies. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta: the pixel, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Web3Forms: sending the contact form.

How to limit ads

You can choose which ads you see from your Facebook or Instagram account, under Accounts Center → Ad preferences. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under Uruguay’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law 18.331), you can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your data. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page.
