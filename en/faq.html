Google review NFC stand

What is the Google review NFC stand?

A tabletop stand with an NFC chip. Customers hold their phone to it and the page to leave your business a Google review opens right away, with no searching or typing links.

How much does the NFC stand cost?

It costs $1,200 Uruguayan pesos per stand, or $3,000 for the 3-pack ($1,000 each). We deliver it programmed with your business’s review link. See the stand.

Do you ship outside Uruguay?

Not for now: we ship anywhere in Uruguay, in 1 to 3 business days. Shipping is paid by the buyer and we arrange it on WhatsApp. Outside Uruguay, you can still get your review link and its QR code for free with our generator.

What does “programmed with my link” mean?

Before delivery, we write your Google Business Profile’s review link to the chip. It arrives ready to use: set it on the counter and it starts working.

How do I buy the NFC stand?

Go to the product page, choose 1 stand or the 3-pack and the quantity, and tap “Buy on WhatsApp”. Send us your business name or your Google profile link and we’ll arrange delivery.

Can I return the stand?

Yes. You have 5 business days from delivery to return it. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll arrange it.

Does it work with any phone?

It works with most current phones that have NFC: iPhone XS or later and Android phones with NFC turned on. No app needed.

Does the stand need batteries, charging or an internet connection?

No. The NFC chip is passive: it uses no battery or power. Only your customer’s phone needs an internet connection to open the review page.

What do I need for it to work?

A Google Business Profile for your business (the one that shows up on Google Maps). If you don’t have one yet, message us and we’ll sort it out together.

What does the stand say?

It’s printed in Spanish: “Deje una reseña · Toque aquí” (“Leave a review · Tap here”), with the NFC icon, the Google logo and five stars.

Why should I get more Google reviews?

Reviews build trust with people who search for you, and they’re one of the signals Google uses to show local businesses in Search and on Google Maps. The easier they are to leave, the more reviews you’ll get.

If I bought the stand, do I get a discount on my website?

Yes. If you build your website with Bweb within 60 days of buying the stand, we’ll take $1,200 UYU off.
