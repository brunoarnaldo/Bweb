Web design

Do you build websites?

Yes. We design, maintain and rank websites for small businesses in Uruguay, Latin America and beyond. You can see some of them in Projects, along with what each one set out to do.

Do you work with businesses outside Uruguay?

Yes. We’re based in Uruguay and work remotely: everything is handled online, on WhatsApp or by email. And our time zone (GMT-3) overlaps with business hours in the US and Canada.

Do I need to know about tech to have a website?

No. We explain everything in plain English and do the work for you.

How long until my website is ready?

Days, not months. We show you the progress along the way.

What does web design include?

Professional custom design, responsive design (it looks great on phones, tablets and computers), persuasive copy, speed optimization and a contact form.

Maintenance

What does website maintenance include?

Automatic backups, 24/7 uptime monitoring, bug fixes and content changes included.

What happens if my website goes down or breaks?

With maintenance, we monitor your site 24/7 and fix anything that goes wrong. Zero headaches.

SEO

How do you help me show up on Google?

With an initial technical audit, on-page optimization and content built to rank. Every month we send you a ranking report.

How do I know SEO is working?

We send you monthly ranking reports so you can see how your business is doing on Google.

Pricing and hiring

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs. Message us on WhatsApp or fill out the form and we’ll send you a custom proposal within 24 hours, no strings attached.

Is the website review free?

Yes. We look at your current website and tell you what to improve, free and with no commitment.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. No fine print, no endless contracts.
