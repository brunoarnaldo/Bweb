Acá Cripto

Minas, Lavalleja · nationwide

The goal

It started with a simple question: if I have crypto, where can I spend it in Uruguay? The idea was to bring together in one place the businesses that accept crypto payments, with reliable information and at no cost for businesses to join.

How we built it

  • Interactive map with filters by city, category and cryptocurrency, a search bar and a “Near me” button to see what’s around.
  • A profile for each business with its verification date: listings are checked by hand and re-checked every six months.
  • An “Add your business” form so any business can apply for free.
  • City pages and guides to learn how to pay and get paid in crypto.
  • A printable sign and an onboarding service for businesses that want to start accepting crypto.

La Barraquita

Minas, Lavalleja

The goal

La Barraquita is a family business in Minas since 2002. The goal was to bring its whole catalog online, with prices in plain sight, so customers across the country can browse and order easily, and to handle wholesale orders separately.

How we built it

  • A catalog of more than 100 products with prices, organized into dogs, cats, farm, grains, animal health and flours, with search and sorting by price or name.
  • A carousel with the month’s deals: tap a deal and order it straight on WhatsApp.
  • A cart to build the order and a “Wholesale” button that opens WhatsApp with the wholesale inquiry already written.
  • Trust-building information up front: approval from Uruguay’s Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries (MGAP), same-day delivery with their own fleet, and payment methods.
  • Guides and tips, an FAQ and an exchange and returns policy.

GRADIN

Toledo, Canelones · Diamant Maquinaria

The goal

When buying machinery, customers need to understand quickly which equipment suits them, how much it costs and how to pay for it. The goal was to show each machine with its price, specs and in-house financing, turn every visit into a WhatsApp quote request, and take reservations for equipment that hasn’t arrived yet.

How we built it

  • A catalog you can filter by brand (GRADIN, TITAN and HSY LIFT), with prices in US dollars and key specs: height, load capacity and power source.
  • An in-house financing section (50% down and the rest in up to 18 installments) with a button to simulate it.
  • A “Coming soon · reserve now” status to sell equipment before it arrives.
  • “Get a quote” and WhatsApp buttons always in view, plus a form with the equipment of interest.
  • Store details at hand: the address in Toledo, opening hours and directions with Google Maps.
