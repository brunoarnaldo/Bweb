    Your official link in a minute

    Google already gives you the link in your Business Profile. You just need to know where to look.

    1. 1

      Search for your business on Google

      Signed in with the account that manages your profile, search for your business name or go to business.google.com.

    2. 2

      Tap “Ask for reviews”

      It’s in your profile’s panel. Sometimes it shows up as “Get more reviews”.

    3. 3

      Copy the link and paste it above

      It starts with g.page/r/. Paste it into the generator and we’ll create the QR code.

    Don’t manage the profile? Look up the business in Google’s Place ID Finder and paste the code that starts with ChIJ. Stuck? Message us and we’ll get it for you.

    What is a Google review link?

    It’s a link that opens the window to leave your business a Google review right away. Your customers don’t have to search for you on Google Maps or find the reviews button.

    Is the generator free?

    Yes. Creating the link and downloading the QR code is free, and you don’t need to sign up.

    Where should I use the review link?

    Send it on WhatsApp after every sale, add it to your Instagram bio, your email signature or your invoices. You can print the QR code and leave it at the register or on tables.

    What’s the difference between the QR code and the NFC stand?

    With the QR code, your customer opens the camera and scans it. With the NFC stand, they just hold their phone to it and the review opens. The Bweb stand comes programmed with your link, ready for the counter, for $1,200 UYU (shipping within Uruguay).

    Can I offer a discount in exchange for a review?

    No. Google’s policies prohibit offering discounts, gifts or payments in exchange for reviews, and those reviews can be removed. What works is asking at the right moment and making it easy with the link.

    Do I need a Google Business Profile?

    Yes. The link goes to your Business Profile’s reviews, the one that shows up on Google Maps. If you don’t have one yet, message us and we’ll sort it out together.
